# My Books

A personal collection of some books I've always wanted to (but unfortunately never yet found the time to) read, kept here for backup and easy access.

## Science, Futurism & Technology

- **Hyperspace**, **Parallel Worlds**, **Physics of the Impossible**, **Physics of the Future**, **The Future of Humanity**, **Visions** — Michio Kaku. Popular-science explorations of theoretical physics, extra dimensions, and where technology and civilization may be headed.
- **The Singularity Is Near**, **The Singularity Is Nearer** — Ray Kurzweil. On accelerating technological change and the future of artificial intelligence.
- **Abundance** — Peter Diamandis. Argues that converging exponential technologies can solve humanity's grand challenges.
- **Exponential Organizations** — Salim Ismail. How organizations can leverage exponential technologies to scale rapidly.
- **Factfulness** — Hans Rosling. Uses data to challenge common misconceptions about the state of the world.
- **The Brain** — David Eagleman. A tour of neuroscience and how the brain shapes perception and identity.

## Fantasy — The Inheritance Cycle

- **Eragon**, **Eldest**, **Brisingr**, **Inheritance**, **The Fork, the Witch, and the Worm** — Christopher Paolini. Epic fantasy series following a young dragon rider.

## Memoirs

- **Playing It My Way** — Sachin Tendulkar. Autobiography of the cricketer.
- **The Test of My Life** — Yuvraj Singh. Memoir covering his cricket career and cancer recovery.

## Literary Fiction

- **The Kite Runner** — Khaled Hosseini. A novel of friendship, guilt, and redemption set against Afghanistan's recent history.

## Economics & Environment

- **The Carbohydrate Economy** — David Morris & Irshad Ahmed. On building an economy around plant-based materials and fuels instead of petroleum.
